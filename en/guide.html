Guide

Prepare your transport, step by step.

Check your gauge, measure your cargo and gather the right information. A well-prepared request means a faster quote and a convoy with no surprises.

Check

Is your shipment an abnormal load?

Enter the measurements of your cargo. The result is indicative: we confirm it at survey stage, once the cargo is on the trailer.

Limits under Decree no. 2-10-421: width 2.55 m, height 4 m, length 16.50 m for an articulated vehicle, 40 t gross combination weight. Categories: Autoroutes du Maroc.

Measuring

Four measurements, taken in the right place.

A ten-centimetre error can change the trailer or the route. Measure the cargo ready to travel, packaging and supports included.

Length

Overall

Measure from end to end, protruding parts included: flanges, lifting eyes, supports.

Width

At the widest point

This is the measurement that most often triggers a permit and marking.

Height

From the ground to the top

Give the height of the cargo alone. We add the height of the trailer deck.

Weight

Actual, not estimated

Use the manufacturer’s plate or the weighbridge ticket. State the lifting points and centre of gravity if known.

Send what you have, even if it is incomplete. Each extra detail saves a round of questions.

Glossary

The vocabulary of abnormal loads.

The terms you will come across in a quote or a permit.

Gauge
Maximum dimensions permitted on the road for a vehicle and its load.
Indivisible load
A load that cannot be dismantled or split without damage or excessive cost. It is what justifies an abnormal-load movement.
Abnormal load
A road combination whose dimensions or weight exceed the highway code limits and which travels under a permit.
Low-loader
A low-deck trailer designed for plant and for tall or heavy machines.
Lashing
Securing the cargo to the trailer with chains, straps and chocks so that it does not move under braking or in bends.
Escort vehicle
A vehicle that travels ahead of or behind the convoy to warn other road users.
Gross combination weight
The weight of the truck, the trailer and the load together.
Structure
A bridge, viaduct or tunnel. Each has a weight and height limit to be checked along the route.
Route survey
Checking the journey before the transport: clearances, bends, gradients, access.
Delivery note
The document signed on arrival by the consignee. It confirms delivery and records any reservations.
