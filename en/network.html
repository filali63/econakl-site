Before departure

What makes a route passable.

A good route cannot be read off a map. It has to be checked, from the loading point to the delivery site.

01

Survey

Bridges, clearances, bends, roadworks and town crossings are checked along the route.

02

Permits

We support you with abnormal-load permits and keep to the required travel times.

03

Escort

When the dimensions require it, the escort is planned as part of preparing the convoy.

04

Site access

Entrance, manoeuvring area and unloading are checked with the site manager.
