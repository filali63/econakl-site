Frequently asked questions

What we are asked before a quote.

If your question is not here, ask it in the form or by email.

What information do you need for a quote?

The minimum: what you are shipping, length × width × height, weight, pick-up point, delivery point and required date. Photos, a drawing or a data sheet save us time. If you don’t know a value, write “to be confirmed”: we will help you pin it down.

When does a shipment count as an abnormal load in Morocco?

As soon as the load exceeds the dimensions or weights permitted on the road. A permit, a defined route and sometimes an escort are then required. We check your specific case from the actual dimensions, including the trailer.

Do you have your own trucks?

Yes. We haul with our own trucks and low-loaders, driven by our drivers. The equipment is chosen to suit the dimensions and weight of your load.

Who handles permits and escorts?

We support you with abnormal-load permits and plan the escort when the route requires one. What is included is set out in the quote.

Can you collect cargo from the port?

Yes: we collect your cargo at Tanger Med, Casablanca or another Moroccan port and deliver it to site. For customs clearance, we work with your freight forwarder.

Where do you operate?

Throughout Morocco: city to city, from ports to factories and construction sites, all the way to remote sites in the South.

When will I get a price?

For an abnormal load, the final price often depends on the route survey and the permits. In our first reply, we tell you what still needs to be checked.
