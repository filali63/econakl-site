Abnormal loads

Abnormal loads in Morocco, from survey to delivery.

When a cargo exceeds the road gauge, everything is decided before departure. We survey the route, support you with the permit and deliver with our own trucks and drivers.

The rules

When does a shipment become an abnormal load?

As soon as the loaded convoy exceeds one of these four limits. A single one is enough.

Road gauge limits in Morocco
MeasurementRoad limitBeyond it
Width2.55 mPermit, marking, sometimes an escort
Height4.00 m, load includedRoute checked: bridges, lines, gantries
Length16.50 m for an articulated vehicle, 18.75 m for a road trainPermit and a route suited to the bends
Gross combination weight40 t for a 5- or 6-axle combinationPermit and structures checked

Source: Decree no. 2-10-421 on vehicles (Articles 3 and 5). These limits apply to the loaded convoy, not to the cargo alone.

The categories

Three categories, three levels of preparation.

The longer, wider or heavier the convoy, the more demanding the survey and the travel conditions.

Frequent questions

What we are asked about abnormal loads.

Is your case unusual? Write to us and we will look at your measurements.

My cargo is only slightly over. Is it still an abnormal load?

Yes. As soon as a limit is exceeded, even by a few centimetres, the transport falls under the abnormal-load rules. A different trailer sometimes keeps it within the gauge: we check this at survey stage.

How far ahead should we start?

As early as possible. For the motorway, the permit application must be filed at least one week in advance. The route survey and site preparation come on top of that.

Is an escort always required?

No. It depends on the category of the convoy and on the route. We state it in the quote.

Can the cargo be dismantled to avoid an abnormal load?

If the cargo can be dismantled without damage or excessive cost, that is often the simplest solution. Abnormal-load transport is intended for indivisible loads.
