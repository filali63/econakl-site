How we work

An abnormal load cannot be improvised.

We remove the usual causes of delay: a truck kept waiting, an impassable route, a missing permit, cargo not ready at the supplier’s.

01

Study the load

Dimensions, weight, lifting points, site access and required date are checked before choosing the truck.

02

Survey the route

Bridges, clearances, bends, roadworks and permitted travel times are checked along the whole route.

03

Prepare the convoy

Truck, driver, escort, permits and documents are brought together by a single contact.

04

Deliver and close

Signed delivery note, photos and documents are handed over in a single file.
