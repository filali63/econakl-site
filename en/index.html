Before the quote

Four measurements decide the whole convoy.

With the length, width, height and weight of your load, we choose the trailer, check the route and know whether a permit or an escort is needed.

  1. A

    Length

    It sets the trailer type and how the convoy takes bends and roundabouts.

  2. B

    Width

    It determines the road space taken, the marking and the need for an escort.

  3. C

    Height

    Load included, it governs clearance under bridges and overhead lines.

  4. D

    Weight

    It decides the number of axles and which structures the convoy can cross.

Our method

An abnormal load is prepared before it moves.

A poorly prepared convoy gets stuck at the first bridge. We prepare every shipment in four stages.

01

One file

Cargo, dates, constraints, documents, site access and who does what: everything is written down in one place.

02

The right route

We compare the possible routes by bridges, clearances, cost and lead time.

03

A prepared convoy

Truck, driver, escort, permits and loading are arranged by the same contact.

04

A complete file

Signed delivery note, photos and documents are handed over after delivery.
