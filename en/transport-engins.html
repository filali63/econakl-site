Plant transport

Your construction plant, from one site to the next.

Excavator, loader or mobile crane: our low-loaders move your plant across Morocco, with loading and lashing checked.

How we do it

A machine is transported the way it is driven: methodically.

A badly loaded machine damages the trailer, the road or itself. Here is how we proceed.

01

Choose the low-loader

According to the weight, the track or wheel width and the height of the machine with the boom folded.

02

Load

Up the ramps if the machine runs, by lifting if it has broken down or been dismantled.

03

Lash

Chains and tensioners on the manufacturer’s anchor points, bucket and boom lowered.

04

Deliver

Unloading on site, at the spot agreed with the site manager.
