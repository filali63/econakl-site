Service futur

Logistique e-commerce pour la nouvelle génération du commerce marocain.

ECONAKL prépare un modèle dédié aux vendeurs en ligne : préparation commandes, livraison urbaine, retours et visibilité opérationnelle.

The opportunity

Online sales need more than delivery. They need rhythm.

The best e-commerce logistics operators focus on inventory accuracy, fast handoff, delivery visibility, reverse logistics and customer communication. ECONAKL will adapt these principles to Moroccan sellers and regional growth.

Fulfillment

Reception, storage, picking, packing logic and handover discipline for growing stores.

Delivery

Operational lanes for Casablanca, Rabat, Tangier, Marrakech, Agadir and other cities.

Returns

Control reverse logistics

Failed delivery, customer returns and restocking workflows designed before volume grows.
